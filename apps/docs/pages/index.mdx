# Welcome to Agevine

Agevine is the open-source operating system for eldercare AI. It allows you to unify health data from smartwatches and AI Voice Callers into a single, beautifully designed dashboard.

## Why Agevine?

Modern healthcare APIs are fragmented. You have to integrate Apple HealthKit, Google Fit, Garmin, Fitbit, and 10 different AI voice providers just to build a simple dashboard.

Agevine provides a single unified API and pre-built SDKs to push data into a unified dashboard. 

## SDKs

- **@agevine/voice**: Integrate Retell AI, Vapi, and Bland AI callers.
- **@agevine/wearables**: Stream live vitals from Apple Watch and Fitbit.
